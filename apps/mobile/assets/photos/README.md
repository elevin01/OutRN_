# Representative photos

Shown only when a place has no photo of its own or none of its own loads, always labelled
"Representative photo · not this place" with their credit (`src/lib/representative.ts`). They illustrate a kind of place, never a
specific venue. A restaurant or a cafe whose cuisine has a photo here shows that first (a pizzeria,
pizza). Demo mode (`EXPO_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=true`) also uses them beside its invented posts. The web
app ships the same files in `packages/web/public/representative/`.

To add a category: add freely licensed photos here (Unsplash License, CC0 or public domain; CC BY
only with its author and license in the credit), list them above, and map them in
`src/lib/representative.ts` and the web app's `lib/categories.ts`. Keep the label and credit. Never
map a photo of a real, named place: nothing in the picture (a sign, a name, a landmark) may say which
place it is. A Commons file's title may name where it was taken; the picture must not.
